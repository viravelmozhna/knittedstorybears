**ABBREVIATION:**  
ch - chain  
st - stitch  
sc - single crochet  
inc - increase  
dec - decrease  
BLO - back loops only  

## Head

*Start crochet with smooth yarn.*

**RND 1**: Ch 2 *(pic.1)*, 6 sc in second ch from a hook *(pic.2-3)* \[6\]

**RND 2**: inc in all 6 st \[12\]

**RND 3**: (sc in next st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times *(pic.4)* \[18\]

**RND 4-7**: sc in next 18 st \[18\] - *4 rounds* *(pic.5)*

**RND 8**: inc in next 6 st, sc in next 12 st *(pic.6)* \[24\]

**RND 9**: (sc in next st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times, sc in next 12 st *(pic.7)* \[30\]

**RND 10-11**: sc in next 30 st \[30\] - *2 rounds* *(pic.8)*  
*Change to the fluffy yarn* *(pic.9)*

**RND 12**: (sc in next 4 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[36\]  
*Put the safety eyes between Rnd 9 and 10* *(pic.14-15)*  

**RND 13**: (sc in next 5 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[42\]

**RND 14**: (sc in next 6 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[48\]

**RND 15-19**: sc in next 48 st \[48\] - *5 rounds*  
*Stuff the head and continue stuffing as you go.*

**RND 20**: (sc in next 6 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[42\]

**RND 21**: (sc in next 5 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[36\]

**RND 22**: (sc in next 4 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[30\]

**RND 23**: (sc in next 3 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[24\]

**RND 22**: (sc in next 2 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[18\]

**RND 23**: (sc in next st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[12\]

**RND 24**: dec 6 times \[6\]. *Fasten off and weave in the yarn end.*  

## Ears

*Make 2.*  

*Crochet with smooth yarn.*

**RND 1**: Ch 2, 6 sc in second ch from a hook \[6\]

**RND 2**: (sc in next 2 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 2 times \[8\]

**RND 3**: (sc in next 3 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 2 times \[10\]

**RND 4**: (sc in next 4 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 2 times \[12\]

**RND 5**: (sc in next 5 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 2 times \[14\]

**RND 6**: (sc in next 6 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 2 times \[16\]

**RND 7**: (sc in next 7 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 2 times \[18\]

**RND 8**: (sc in next 8 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 2 times \[20\]

**RND 9-12**: sc in next 20 st \[20\] - *4 rounds*  
*The ears do not need to be stuffed.  
Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.**Sew the ears to the head* *(pic.20-22)*

## Body

*Crochet with fluffy yarn.*

**RND 1**: Ch 2, 6 sc in second ch from a hook \[6\]

**RND 2**: inc in all 6 st \[12\]

**RND 3**: (sc in next st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[18\]

**RND 4**: (sc in next 2 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[24\]

**RND 5**: (sc in next 3 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[30\]

**RND 6**: sc in next 30 st \[30\]

**RND 7**: (sc in next 4 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[36\]

**RND 8-22**: sc in next 16 st \[36\] - *15 rounds*

**RND 23**: (sc in next 4 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[30\]

**RND 24**: sc in next 30 st \[30\]  
*Stuff the body and continue stuffing as you go.*

**RND 25**: (sc in next 3 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[24\]

**RND 26**: (sc in next 2 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[18\]

**RND 27**: (sc in next st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[12\]

**RND 28**: dec 6 times \[6\]. *Fasten off and weave in the yarn end.*  

## Neck

*Crochet with fluffy yarn.*  
Ch 25.  
*Start crochet in the first ch and work in rounds.*

**RND 1**: sc in all 25 ch \[25\]

**RND 2-5**: sc in next 25 st \[25\] - *4 rounds* *(pic.25)*

**RND 6**: sc in next 23 st, dec \[24\]

**RND 7-8**: sc in next 24 st \[24\] - *2 rounds* *(pic.26)*  
*Work in rows.*

**ROW 9**: Ch 1, turn the detail *(pic.27)*, sc in next 12 st *(pic.28)*

**ROW 10**: Ch 1, turn the detail *(pic.29)*, sc in next 12 st  
*Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.*  

*Sew the neck to the head.* *(pic.32-33)*  
*Stuff the neck.* *(pic.34)*  
*Sew the body to the neck.* *(pic.36-37)*

## Legs

*Make 4.*  
*Crochet with smooth yarn.*

**RND 1**: Ch 2, 6 sc in the second chain from a hook \[6\]

**RND 2**: sc in next 6 st \[6\]

**RND 3**: (sc in next st, inc in next st) \* repeat 3 times \[9\]

**RND 4**: (sc in next 2 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 3 times \[12\]

**RND 5-19**: sc in next 12 st \[12\] - *15 rounds*  
*Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.  
Stuff the legs.**Sew legs to the body* *(pic.39)*.  

## Tail

*Crochet with smooth yarn.***RND** **1**: Ch 2, 4 sc in second ch from a hook \[4\]

**RND 2**: sc in next 3 st, inc in next st \[5\]

**RND 3**: sc in next 4 st, inc in next st \[6\]

**RND 4**: sc in next 5 st, inc in next st \[7\]

**RND 5**: sc in next 6 st, inc in next st \[8\]  
*Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.  
The tail does not need to be stuffed.**Sew the tail to the body* *(pic.39)*.  

## Scarf

Ch 130 *(pic.40)*.  
*Start crochet in the second ch from a hook.  
Crochet in rows.*

**ROW 1**: sc in next 129 st *(pic.41)*. Ch 1, turn the detail *(pic.42-43)*

**ROW 2-5**: BLO *(pic.44-45)* sc in next 129 st. Ch 1, turn the detail - *4 rounds*

**ROW 6**: BLO sc in next 129 st. *Fasten off, weave in the yarn ends.*  

*Make 2 little pom poms and sew them to the scarf.*  

**Your llama-alpaca is ready!**
