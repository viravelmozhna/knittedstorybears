As I promised I continue to publish the free Xmas patterns for you.  

Are you ready to crochet more Christmas tree ornaments?  

Let's go! Today is a day for a small cute Scandinavian gnome!  

**ABBREVIATIONS**

*The pattern is written in English, using US terminology.*  

ch - chain  
sc - single crochet  
inc - increase  
dec - decrease  
sl st - slip stitch  
rsc - reverse single crochet  

*You should know how to crochet: chain, slip stitch, single crochet, increase, decrease, reverse single crochet, crochet in rows and rounds.*  

Happy crocheting!  

## Body

*Crochet in grey or red yarn (for your choice).*  
Ch 9.

**ROW 1**: *start crochet in a second ch from a hook* *(pic.1)*, sc in next 8 ch \[8\] *(pic.2)*

**ROW 2**: ch 1 *(pic.3)*, *turn the detail* *(pic.4)*, inc in next st, sc in next 6 st, inc in next st \[10\] *(pic.5)*

**ROW 3**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 10 st \[10\] *(pic.6)*

**ROW 5**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 12 st \[12\]

**ROW 6**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, inc in next st, sc in next 10 st, inc in next st \[14\]

**ROW 7-8**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 14 st \[14\] - *2 rows*

**ROW 9**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, inc in next st, sc in next 12 st, inc in next st \[16\]

**ROW 14**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, inc in next st, sc in next 16 st, inc in next st \[20\]

**ROW 15**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 20 st \[20\]

**ROW 16**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, inc in next st, sc in next 18 st, inc in next st \[22\]

**ROW 17-19**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 22 st \[22\] - *3 rows (pic.7)*

**ROW 20**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec *(pic.8-9)*, sc in next 18 st, dec *(pic.10-11)* \[20\]

**ROW 21**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 20 st \[20\] *(pic.12)*

**ROW 22a**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 5 st, *do not finish this row* \[6\] *(pic.13)*

**ROW 23a**: ch 1 *(pic.14)*, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 4 st \[5\] *(pic.15)*  

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornament-crochet-gnome/06.jpg)

**ROW 24a**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 3 st \[4\]

**ROW 25a**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 2 st \[3\] *(pic.16)*  
*Fasten off.*  

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornament-crochet-gnome/07.jpg)

***Start crochet in the last stitch of row 21, it will be the first stitch now (pic.17-18).***

**ROW 22b**: dec *(pic.19-22)*, sc in next 5 st, *do not finish this row* \[6\] *(pic. 23)*  

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornament-crochet-gnome/08.jpg)

**ROW 23b**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 4 st \[5\]

**ROW 24b**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 3 st \[4\]

**ROW 25b**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 2 st \[3\] *(pic.24)*  
*Fasten off.*  

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornament-crochet-gnome/09.jpg)

Make 2 pc. *(pic.25)*  

Place both details together, matching up all sides.  
Begin attaching details together with reverse sc around, using cream color yarn. *(pic. 26-34)*  

Stop in the middle of the last side, stuff the detail *(pic.35)*. Continue crochet rsc and close with rsc in first st and fasten off, weave in yarn ends *(pic.36-40)*.

## Nose

*Crochet with yellow yarn.*  
Ch 4.

**RND 1**: *start crochet in a second ch from a hook* *(pic. 79)*, sc in next 2 ch *(pic.80)*, 3 sc in next ch *(pic.81)*, *continue crochet on the other side of foundation chains* *(pic.82)*, sc in next st, 2 sc in next st \[8\] *(pic.83)*  

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornament-crochet-gnome/28.jpg)

**RND 2**: inc in next st, sc in next 2 st, inc in next 2 st, sc in next 2 st, inc in next st \[12\] *(pic.84)*

**RND 3**: sc in next 12 st \[12\] *(pic.85-86)*  

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornament-crochet-gnome/29.jpg)

**RND 4**: dec, sc in next 2 st, dec 2 times, sc in next 2 st, dec \[8\]  
*Stuff the detail*. *(pic.87)*

**RND 5**: dec 4 times \[4\] *(pic.88)*  
*Fasten off, leave a long tail for sewing.*  
Then sew the nose between rounds 12-15 of the body. *(pic.89-90)*  

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornament-crochet-gnome/30.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornament-crochet-gnome/31.jpg)
