Let's go! Today is a day for a crochet gingerbread house!  

**ABBREVIATIONS**

*The pattern is written in English, using US terminology.*  

ch - chain  
sc - single crochet  
BLO - back loops only  
dec - decrease  
sl st - slip stitch  
rsc - reverse single crochet  
dc - double crochet  

*You should know how to crochet: chain, slip stitch, single crochet, decrease, reverse single crochet, crochet in rows and rounds.*  

Happy crocheting!

## Main detail

Crochet in brown yarn.

Ch 23.

ROW 1: start crochet in the second chain from a hook (pic.1), sc in next 22 ch (pic.2) \[22\]

ROW 2-19: ch 1 (pic.3), turn the detail (pic.4), sc in next 22 st (pic.5) \[22\]  

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornaments-crochet-house/02.jpg)

ROW 20: ch 1, turn the detail, dec, sc in next 18 st, dec \[20\] (pic.6)  

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornaments-crochet-house/03.jpg)

ROW 21: ch 1, turn the detail, dec, sc in next 16 st, dec \[18\]

ROW 22: ch 1, turn the detail, dec, sc in next 14 st, dec \[16\]

ROW 23: ch 1, turn the detail, dec, sc in next 12 st, dec \[14\]  
Fasten off, weave in yarn ends.

Make 2 p. (pic.7)

Place both details together, matching up all sides.

Begin attaching details together with reverse sc around, using cream color yarn. (pic.8-18)  

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornaments-crochet-house/04.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornaments-crochet-house/05.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornaments-crochet-house/06.jpg)

Stop in the middle of the last side, stuff the detail (pic.19). Continue crochet rsc and close with rsc in first st and tie off, weave in yarn ends. (pic.20-21)

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornaments-crochet-house/07.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornaments-crochet-house/08.jpg)

## Roof

Crochet in cream yarn.

Don’t count the stitches in these details, you need just to follow the pattern.

Ch 24 (pic.22).

ROW 1: start crochet in the second ch from a hook (pic.23), BLO sc in next 21 ch (pic.24)

ROW 2: ch 1 (pic.25), turn the detail (pic.26), BLO (pic.27) sc in next 20 st (pic.28)

ROW 3: ch 1 (pic.29), turn the detail, BLO sc in next 18 st (pic.30)

ROW 4: ch 1, turn the detail, BLO sc in next 17 st

ROW 5: ch 1, turn the detail, BLO sc in next 15 st

ROW 6: ch 1, turn the detail, BLO sc in next 14 st

ROW 7: ch 1, turn the detail, BLO sc in next 12 st

ROW 8: ch 1, turn the detail, BLO sc in next 11 st  

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornaments-crochet-house/09.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornaments-crochet-house/10.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornaments-crochet-house/11.jpg)

ROW 9: ch 1, turn the detail, BLO sc in next 9 st

ROW 10: ch 1, turn the detail, BLO sc in next 8 st

ROW 11: ch 1, turn the detail, BLO sc in next 6 st

ROW 12: ch 1, turn the detail, BLO sc in next 5 st

ROW 13: ch 1, turn the detail, BLO sc in next 3 st

ROW 14: ch 1, turn the detail, BLO sc in next 2 st (pic.31)

ROW 15: ch 1 (pic.32), turn the detail (pic.33), work in missed stitches of every round (pic.34-35), sc in next 16 st  

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornaments-crochet-house/12.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornaments-crochet-house/13.jpg)

Fasten off, weave in yarn ends.

Make 4 p. (pic.36-37)

Work with 2 details. Place them together with the last row to each other (pic.38). Attaching them with sl st following the outside loops of the stitches (pic.39-47).  

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornaments-crochet-house/14.jpg)

Fasten off, weave in yarn ends.

Repeat for the other two details.

Then attaching these two big details to each other with rsc (pic 48-49).  

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornaments-crochet-house/15.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornaments-crochet-house/16.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornaments-crochet-house/17.jpg)

## Windows

Crochet in cream yarn.

Ch 7 (pic.50).

ROW 1: sl st in next 6 ch \[6\] (pic.51)  

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornaments-crochet-house/18.jpg)

ROW 2-6: ch 1 (pic.52), turn the detail, BLO (pic.53) sl st in next 6 st \[6\] (pic.54-55)

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornaments-crochet-house/19.jpg)

Make 2 p. (pic.56)

Fasten off, weave in yarn ends.

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornaments-crochet-house/20.jpg)

## Door

Crochet in red yarn.

Ch 9 (pic.57).

Start crochet in 4th ch from a hook. Dc in next 5 st (pic.58-59), 4 dc in next st (pic.60), continue on the other side of foundation chains (pic.61), dc in next 4 st, 2 dc in next st (pic.62), ch 1 (pic.63) \[15\]

Crochet rsc around the detail (pic.64).

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornaments-crochet-house/21.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/christmas-tree-ornaments-crochet-house/22.jpg)

## Assembly

Sew the roof to the main detail.

Sew the windows. (pic.65)

Sew the door.

Embroider the windows with red yarn. (pic.66)

Embroider several french knots on the roof. (pic.67-72)
