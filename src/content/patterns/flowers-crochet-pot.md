It is time for autumn and Halloween crochet!  

![](/images/patterns/flowers-crochet-pot/01.jpg)

**I made my crochet holder for the flowers' pot with sizes**:  
height ↠ 2.5 inches / 6.2 cm  
diameter ↠ 2.75 inches / 6.8 cm  
**You can make this holder for the pots with different size**, of course. Just made the size of the detail which will fit your flowers' pot.

**Abbreviations**:  
ch - chain  
sl st - slip stitch  
sc - single crochet  
inc - increase  

## Main detail

Make 15 chains \[15\]  

![](/images/patterns/flowers-crochet-pot/03.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/flowers-crochet-pot/04.jpg)

*(If you make the holder for another size of the pot make the chains till you get a needed height (height of pot + 1/3 of the height of pot))*  
Start in the second ch from a hook, make a slip stitch in all chains \[14\]  

![](/images/patterns/flowers-crochet-pot/05.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/flowers-crochet-pot/06.jpg)

Make 1 chain, turn the detail  

![](/images/patterns/flowers-crochet-pot/07.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/flowers-crochet-pot/08.jpg)

STARTING NOW - WORK IN BACK LOOPS ONLY  
Make a slip stitch in all stitches \[14\]  

![](/images/patterns/flowers-crochet-pot/09.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/flowers-crochet-pot/10.jpg)

Repeat the pattern till the detail wrap the pot  

![](/images/patterns/flowers-crochet-pot/11.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/flowers-crochet-pot/12.jpg)

sew two sides of detail together (with the crochet hook and slip stitches)  

![](/images/patterns/flowers-crochet-pot/13.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/flowers-crochet-pot/14.jpg)

## Bottom circle

RND 1: 2 ch, 6 sc in second ch from a hook \[6\]

RND 2: inc in all stitches \[12\]

RND 3: (sc in next st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[18\]

RND 4: (sc in next 2 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[24\]

RND 5: (sc in next 3 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[30\]

RND 6: (sc in next 4 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[36\]  

*(If you make the holder for another size of hot, crochet it till your circle will fit the main detail)*  

![](/images/patterns/flowers-crochet-pot/15.jpg)

## Assembly

sew the details together (with the crochet hook and slip stitches)
