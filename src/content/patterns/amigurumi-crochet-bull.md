**Terms and stitches.**

*The pattern is written in English, using US terminology.*  
**ch** - chain  
**sl st** - slip stitch  
**sc** - single crochet  
**inc** - increase  
**dec** - (invisible) decrease  
**BLO** - back loops only

## Arms

*Make 2.*  
*Start crochet with brown yarn.*

**RND 1**: 2 ch, 6 sc in second ch from a hook \[6\] /pic.76/

**RND 2-12**: 6 sc \[6\] -- 11 rounds /pic.77/  
*Change yarn to black.*

**RND 13**: (1 sc, 1 inc) \* 3 times \[9\]

**RND 14**: 9 sc \[9\]

**RND 15**: **BLO** (1 sc, 1 dec) \* 3 times \[6\]

*Fasten off, leaving a tail approx. 6 inches (15 cm).*  
*The arms do not need to be stuffed.*

With a yarn needle, insert the tail of the yarn through the front loops of all stitches of the last round. Pull all together at once.

Insert the needle into the arm and pull it out on the border of colors (like you did for the legs /pic.38-43/. Pull tightly. Then cover the arm with yarn and insert the needle and pull it out in the same places where you did that in the previous step. Pull tightly. *Fasten off, weave in the yarn end*. /pic.78-79/
