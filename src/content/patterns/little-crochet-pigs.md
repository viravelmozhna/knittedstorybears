**ABBREVIATIONS**

The pattern is written in English, using US terminology.  

ch - chain  
sc - single crochet  
inc - increase  
dec - decrease  
sl st - slip stitch  
BLO - back loop only  
\[number\] - number of stitches in the round  
*You should know how to crochet: chain, single crochet, increase, decrease, crochet in rounds, crochet in rows, crochet in back loops only.*  

Happy crocheting!  

## Head and body

*Start at the top of the head.*

**RND 1**: 3 ch, 1 sc in the second ch from hook *(pic.1)*, 3 sc in next st *(pic.2)*, *continue on the other side of the foundation chain*, 2 sc in next st *(pic.3)* \[6\]  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-pigs/04.jpg)

**RND 2**: *(pic.4)* inc in next st, sc in next st, inc in next 2 st, sc in next st, inc in next st \[10\]

**RND 3**: *(pic.5)* inc in next st, sc in next 4 st, inc in next st, sc in next 4 st \[12\]

**RND 4**: (sc in next st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[18\]

**RND 5**: (sc in next 2 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[24\]

**RND 6**: (sc in next 3 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[30\]

**RND 7**: (sc in enxt 4 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[36\]

**RND 8**: (sc in next 5 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[42\]

**RND 9**: (sc in next 20 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 2 times \[44\]

**RND 10**: (sc in next 21 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 2 times \[46\]

**RND 11**: (sc in next 22 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 2 times \[48\] *(pic.6)*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-pigs/05.jpg)

**RND 12-14**: sc in next 48 st \[48\] - *3 rounds*

**RND 15**: (dec, sc in next 22 st) \* repeat 2 times \[46\]

**RND 16**: (dec, sc in next 21 st) \* repeat 2 times \[44\]

**RND 17**: (dec, sc in next 20 st) \* repeat 2 times \[42\]

**RND 18**: (dec, sc in next 19 st) \* repeat 2 times \[40\]

**RND 19**: (dec, sc in next 18 st) \* repeat 2 times \[38\]

**RND 20**: (dec, sc in next 17 st) \* repeat 2 times \[36\]

**RND 21**: (sc in next 4 st. dec) \* repeat 6 times \[30\]

**RND 22**: (sc in next 3 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[24\]

**RND 23**: (sc in next 2 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[18\]

**RND 24**: sc in next 18 st \[18\] *(pic.9)*  
*Stuff the head (pic.10).*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-pigs/06.jpg)

**RND 25**: (inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[20\]

**RND 26**: (sc in next st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[22\]

**RND 27**: (sc in next 2 st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[24\]

**RND 28**: (sc in next 3 st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[26\]

**RND 29**: (sc in next 4 st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[28\]

**RND 30**: (sc in next 5 st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[30\]

**RND 31**: (sc in next 6 st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[32\]

**RND 32**: (sc in next 7 st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[34\]

**RND 33**: (sc in next 8 st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[36\]

**RND 34-39**: sc in next 36 st \[36\] - *6 rounds*

**RND 40**: sc in next 34 st *(+ - 1 st, it should be in the middle of the head, pic.11).* *Do not finish this round. Don't fasten off, continue with leg 1.*  

## Leg 1

**RND 1**: sc in next 18 st, *skip next 18 st* \[18\]

**RND 2**: *start in first st of the previous round,* sc in next 18 st \[18\] *(pic.12)*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-pigs/07.jpg)

**RND 3**: (dec, sc in next 7 st) \* repeat 2 times \[16\]

**RND 4**: (dec, sc in next 6 st) \* repeat 2 times \[14\]

**RND 5**: (dec, sc in next 5 st) \* repeat 2 times \[12\]

**RND 6**: (dec, sc in next 4 st) \* repeat 2 times \[10\]

**RND 7**: *BLO* dec 5 times.  
*Fasten off. Leave a tail approx. 15 cm and weave the yarn end (see pic.13-15)*  
*Stuff the body and leg 1 (pic.16).*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-pigs/08.jpg)

## Leg 2

*Start crochet in the first skipped stitch in Rnd 1 of the Leg 1 (pic.17)*.

**RND 1**: sc in next 18 st \[18\]

**RND 2**: *start in first st of the previous round,* sc in next 18 st \[18\]

**RND 3**: (dec, sc in next 7 st) \* repeat 2 times \[16\]

**RND 4**: (dec, sc in next 6 st) \* repeat 2 times \[14\]

**RND 5**: (dec, sc in next 5 st) \* repeat 2 times \[12\]

**RND 6**: (dec, sc in next 4 st) \* repeat 2 times \[10\]  
*Stuff the leg 2*.

**RND 7**: *BLO* dec 5 times.  
*Fasten off. Leave a tail approx. 15 cm and weave the yarn ends (see pic.13-15, 18-19).*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-pigs/09.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-pigs/10.jpg)

## Arms

*Make 2.*

**RND 1**: 2 ch, 6 sc in second ch from hook \[6\]

**RND 2**: (inc in next st, sc in next 2 st) \* repeat 2 times \[8\]

**RND 3-13**: sc in next 8 st \[8\] - *11 rounds*  
*The arms do not need to be stuffed.*  
*Fasten off. Leave a long tail for sewing.*  

## Ears

*Make 2.*

**RND 1**: 2 ch, 4 sc in second ch from hook \[4\]

**RND 2**: (inc in next st, sc in next st) \* repeat 2 times \[6\]

**RND 3**: (inc in next st, sc in next 2 st) \* repeat 2 times \[8\]

**RND 4**: (inc in next st, sc in next 3 st) \* repeat 2 times \[ 10\]

**RND 5**: (inc in next st, sc in next 4 st) \* repeat 2 times \[12\]

**RND 6**: (inc in next st, sc in next 5 st) \* repeat 2 times \[14\]

**RND 7**: (inc in next st, sc in next 6 st) \* repeat 2 times \[16\]

**RND 8**: sc in next 16 st \[16\]

**RND 9**: (dec, sc in next 6 st) \* repeat 2 times \[14\]

**RND 10**: (dec, sc in next 5 st) \* repeat 2 times \[12\]

**RND 11**: (dec, sc in next 4 st) \* repeat 2 times \[10\]  
*The ears do not need to be stuffed. Fasten off and leave a long tail for sewing.*  

## Nose

**RND 1**: 4 ch, 1 sc in the second ch from hook, 1 sc in next st, 3 sc in next st, *continue on the other side of the foundation chain,* 1 sc in next st, 2 sc in next st \[8\]

**RND 2**: inc in next st, sc in next 2 st, inc in next 2 st, sc in next 2 st, inc in next st \[12\]

**RND 3**: inc in next st, sc in next 5 st, inc in next st, sc in next 5 st \[14\]

**RND 4**: *BLO* sc in next 14 st \[14\]

**RND 5**: inc in next st, sc in next 6 st, inc in next st, sc in next 6 st \[16\]  
*Stuff the detail.*  
*Fasten off and leave a long tail for sewing.*  

## Tail

6 ch, *start in the second ch from hook*, inc in all 5 st. *Fasten off, leave a long tail for sewing.*  

## Assembly

*Sew the ears to the head.*  
*Sew the nose to the head.*  
*Embroider the eyes and nose on the head. (pic.22)*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-pigs/11.jpg)

*Sew the arms to the body.*

*Sew the tail to the body.*

## Crown

**RND 1**: 20 ch

**RND 2**: *start in the first chain of the previous round (pic.23-24)*, sc in next 19 st \[19\] *(pic.25)*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-pigs/12.jpg)

**RND 3**: *BLO* (1 sl st, 3 ch *(pic.26)*, 1 sl st in the third ch from a hook *(pic.27)*, 1 sl st in next st of round 2 *(pic.28)*) \* repeat 10 times.  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-pigs/13.jpg)

*Fasten off.*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-pigs/14.jpg)
