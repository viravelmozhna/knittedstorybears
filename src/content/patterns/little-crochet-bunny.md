Hi! Welcome to my blog full of the free amigurumi patterns!  
Here you can find a crochet pattern for the cutest Easter bunny ever.  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/01.jpg)

**ABBREVIATIONS**

*The pattern is written in English, using US terminology.*  

ch - chain  
sc - single crochet  
inc - increase  
dec - decrease  
sl st - slip stitch  
BLO - back loops only  
FLO - front loops only  
\[number\] - a number of stitches in the round  

*You should know how to crochet: chains, slip stitch, single crochet, increase, decrease, crochet in the back or front loops only, crochet in rounds.*  

Happy crocheting!  

## Head and body

*Start at the top of the head.*

**RND 1**: Ch 3, 1 sc in the second ch from hook *(pic.1)*, 3 sc in next st *(pic.2)*, *continue on the other side of the foundation chains (pic.3)*, inc in next st \[6\]  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/02.jpg)

**RND 2**: inc in next st *(pic.4)*, sc in next st, inc in next 2 st, sc in next st, inc in next st \[10\] *(pic.5)*

**RND 3**: inc in next st, sc in next 4 st, inc in next st, sc in next 4 st \[12\] *(pic.6)*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/03.jpg)

**RND 4**: (sc in next st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[18\]

**RND 5**: (sc in next 2 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[24\]  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/04.jpg)

**RND 6**: (sc in next 3 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[30\]

**RND 7**: (sc in next 4 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[36\]

**RND 8**: (sc in next 5 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[42\]  

**RND 9**: (sc in next 20 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 2 times \[44\]

**RND 10**: (sc in next 21 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 2 times \[46\]

**RND 11**: (sc in next 22 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 2 times \[48\] *(pic.10)*

**RND 12-14**: sc in next 48 st \[48\] - *3 rounds (pic.11)*

**RND 15**: (dec, sc in next 22 st) \* repeat 2 times \[46\]  

**RND 16**: (dec, sc in next 21 st) \* repeat 2 times \[44\]

**RND 17**: (dec, sc in next 20 st) \* repeat 2 times \[42\]

**RND 18**: (dec, sc in next 19 st) \* repeat 2 times \[40\]

**RND 19**: (dec, sc in next 18 st) \* repeat 2 times \[38\]

**RND 20**: (dec, sc in next 17 st) \* repeat 2 times \[36\]

**RND 21**: (sc in next 4 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[30\]

**RND 22**: (sc in next 3 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[24\]

**RND 23**: (sc in next 2 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[18\]

**RND 24**: sc in next 18 st \[18\] *(pic.12)*  
*Stuff the head.*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/05.jpg)

**RND 25**: (inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[20\]

**RND 26**: (sc in next st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[22\]

**RND 27**: (sc in next 2 st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[24\]

**RND 28**: (sc in next 3 st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[26\]  

**RND 29**: (sc in next 4 st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \*repeat 2 times \[28\]

**RND 30**: (sc in next 5 st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[30\]

**RND 31**: (sc in next 6 st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[32\]

**RND 32**: (sc in next 7 st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[34\]

**RND 33**: (sc in next 8 st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[36\]  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/06.jpg)

**RND 34-39**: sc in next 36 st \[36\] - *6 rounds*

**RND 40**: sc in next 34 st (+ - 1 st, it should be in the middle of the head *(pic.16)). Do not finish this round. Don't fasten off, continue with leg 1.*  

## Leg 1

**RND 1**: sc in next 18 st, *skip next 18 st* \[18\]

**RND 2**: *start in first st of the previous round (pic.17)*, sc in next 18 st \[18\]  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/07.jpg)

**RND 3**: (dec, sc in next 7 st) \* repeat 2 times \[16\]

**RND 4**: sc in next 16 st \[16\] *(pic.18)*

**RND 5**: (dec, sc in next 6 st) \* repeat 2 times \[14\]

**RND 6**: sc in next 14 st \[14\]

**RND 7**: (dec, sc in next 5 st) \* repeat 2 times \[12\]

**RND 8**: sc in next 12 st \[12\]

**RND 9**: (dec, sc in next 4 st) \* repeat 2 times \[10\]

**RND 10**: dec 5 times.  
Fasten off. Leave a tail approx. 15 cm. *(pic.19-20)*  
With a sewing needle, insert the end of the yarn through front loops of all stitches of the last round. Pull all together at once and weave in the yarn end. *(pic.21-23)*  
*Stuff the body and leg 1.*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/08.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/09.jpg)

## Leg 2

*Join yarn in the first skipped stitch in RND 1 of the Leg 1 (pic.25).*

**RND 1**: sc in next 18 st \[18\]

**RND 2**: *start in first st of the previous round*, sc in next 18 st \[18\]  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/10.jpg)

**RND 3**: (dec, sc in next 7 st) \* repeat 2 times \[16\] *(pic.26)*

**RND 4**: sc in next 16 st \[16\]

**RND 5**: (dec, sc in next 6 st) \* repeat 2 times \[14\]

**RND 6**: sc in next 14 st \[14\]

**RND 7**: (dec, sc in next 5 st) \* repeat 2 times \[12\]

**RND 8**: sc in next 12 st \[12\]

**RND 9**: (dec, sc in next 4 st) \* repeat 2 times \[10\]  
*Stuff the leg 2. (pic.27)*

**RND 10**: dec 5 times.  
Fasten off. Leave a tail approx. 15 cm.  
With a sewing needle, insert the end of the yarn through front loops of all stitches of the last round.  
Pull all together at once and weave in the yarn end.  
With an additional cut of yarn and needle sew up a hole between the legs. *(pic.28-29)*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/11.jpg)

## Ears (make 2)

**RND 1**: Ch 2 *(pic.31)*, 6 sc in second ch from hook \[6\] *(pic.32)*

**RND 2**: (inc in next st, sc in next st) \* repeat 3 times \[9\]

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/12.jpg)

**RND 3**: sc in next 9 st \[9\] *(pic.34)*

**RND 4**: (inc in next st, sc in next 2 st) \* repeat 3 times \[12\]

**RND 5**: sc in next 12 st \[12\]

**RND 6**: (inc in next st, sc in next 3 st) \* repeat 3 times \[15\]

**RND 7**: sc in next 15 st \[15\]

**RND 8**: (inc in next st, sc in next 4 st) \* repeat 3 times \[18\]

**RND 9-14**: sc in next 18 st \[18\] - *6 rounds (pic.36)*

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/13.jpg)

**RND 15**: (dec, sc in next 4 st) \* repeat 3 times \[15\]

**RND 16**: (dec, sc in next 3 st) \* repeat 3 times \[12\]

*Do not stuff the ears.*

Fasten off and leave a long tail for sewing. *(pic.37)*.  
Fold the circle to a semicircle and then fold it again. With a needle sew up the ears *(pic.38-39)*

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/14.jpg)

## Arms (make 2)

**RND 1**: Ch 2, 6 sc in second ch from hook \[6\] *(pic.41)*

**RND 2**: (inc in next st, sc in next 2 st) \* repeat 2 times \[8\]

**RND 3-13**: sc in next 8 st \[8\] - *11 rounds*  
*Do not stuff the details.*  
Fasten off. Leave a long tail for sewing. *(pic.43)*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/15.jpg)

## Tail

**RND 1**: Ch 2, 6 sc in second ch from hook \[6\] *(pic.44)*

**RND 2**: (inc in next st, sc in next st) \* repeat 3 times \[9\]

**RND 3**: (dec, sc in next st) \* repeat 3 times \[6\]

*Stuff the detail.*

Fasten off, leave a long tail for sewing. *(pic.45)*

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/16.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/17.jpg)

## Flowers (make 3)

*Сrochet with pink yarn*

**RND 1**: Ch 2, 5 sc in second ch from a hook

**RND 2**: ***FLO of round 1***. 1 sl st *(pic.49)*, (3 ch, 2 sl st by the chains *(pic.50)*, 1 sl st in the same loop of round 1, 3 ch, 2 sl st by the chains *(pic.51)*, 1 sl st in the next loop of round 1) \* repeat 4 times, 3 ch, 2 sl st by the chains, 1 sl st in the same loop of round 1, 3 ch, 2 sl st by the chains *(pic.52)*

**RND 3**: ***BLO of round 1*** *(pic.53)*. 1 sl st, (3 ch, 2 sl st by the chains, 1 sl st in the same loop of round 1, 3 ch, 2 sl st by the chains, 1 sl st in the next loop of round 1) \* repeat 4 times, 3 ch, 2 sl st by the chains, 1 sl st in the same loop of round 1, 3 ch, 2 sl st by the chains

*Fasten off.*

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/18.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/19.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/20.jpg)

## Leaves (make 5)

*Сrochet with green yarn (pic.58-60)*

6 ch, *start in the second ch from a hook*, 5 sl st.

*Fasten off.*

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/21.jpg)

## Base

*Сrochet with white yarn*

**RND 1**: Ch 2, 6 sc in second ch from a hook \[6\]

**RND 2**: inc in next 6 st \[12\]

**RND 3**: (sc in next st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[18\]

**RND 4**: (sc in next 2 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[24\] (*pic.61)*

*Fasten off.*

## Assembly

Embroider the nose and eyes on the head as shown on *pics 46-48*.

Put the flowers and leaves to the base as shown on *pics 62-66*.

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/22.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/23.jpg)

Take the felt piece 4.25 x 4.25 inches size.

Fold the felt and glue or sew up the edges of it as shown on *pics 67-70*.

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/24.jpg)

Glue or sew up the flowers to the felt as shown on *pics 71-72*.

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-bunny/25.jpg)
