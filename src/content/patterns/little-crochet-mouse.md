**ABBREVIATIONS**  

The pattern is writting in English, using US terminology.

FOR CROCHETING  
ch - chain

sc - single crochet

inc - increase

dec - decrease  
RND - round  
*You should know how to crochet: chain, single crochet, increase, decrease, crochet in rounds, crochet in rows.*  

FOR KNITTING  
K - knit stitch  
P - purl stitch  
st - stitch

*You should know how to knit with needles: cast on, knit stitch, purl stitch, cast off.*  

## Head and body

*Start at the top of the head.*

*Start with gray yarn.*

**RND 1**: 3 ch *(pic.1)*, 1 sc in second ch from a hook *(pic.2-3)*, 3 sc in next st *(pic.4)*, *continue on the other side of the foundation chains (pic.5)*, 2 sc in next st *(pic.6)* \[6\]

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-mouse/06.jpg)

**RND 2**: *(pic.7)* inc in next st, sc in next st, inc in next 2 st, sc in next st, inc in next st *(pic.8)* \[10\]

**RND 3**: (inc in next st, sc in next 4 st) \* repeat 2 times *(pic.9)* \[12\]  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-mouse/07.jpg)

**RND 4**: (sc in next st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[18\]

**RND 5**: (sc in next 2 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[24\] *(pic.10)*

**RND 6**: (sc in next 3 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[30\]

**RND 7**: (sc in next 4 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[36\]

**RND 8**: (sc in next 5 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[42\] *(pic.11)*

**RND 9**: (sc in next 20 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 2 times \[44\]

**RND 10**: (sc in next 21 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 2 times \[46\]

**RND 11**: (sc in next 22 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 2 times \[48\] *(pic.12)*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-mouse/08.jpg)

**RND 12-14**: sc in next 48 st \[48\] - *3 rounds (pic.13)*

**RND 15**: (dec, sc in next 22 st) \* repeat 2 times \[46\]

**RND 16**: (dec, sc in next 21 st) \* repeat 2 times \[44\]

**RND 17**: (dec, sc in next 20 st) \* repeat 2 times \[42\]  
*If you use the safety eyes put them now between rounds 14 and 15 (5 stitches apart).*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-mouse/09.jpg)

**RND 18**: (dec, sc in next 19 st) \* repeat 2 times \[40\]

**RND 19**: (dec, sc in next 18 st) \* repeat 2 times \[38\]

**RND 20**: (dec, sc in next 17 st) \* repeat 2 times \[36\]

**RND 21**: (sc in next 4 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[30\]

**RND 22**: (sc in next 3 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[24\]  
*Stuff the head and continue stuffing as you go. (pic.15)*

**RND 23**: (sc in next 2 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[18\]

**RND 24**: sc in next 18 st \[18\] *(pic.16)*

**RND 25**: (inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[20\]

**RND 26**: (sc in next st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[22\]

**RND 27**: (sc in next 2 st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[24\]

**RND 28**: (sc in next 3 st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[26\]  

**RND 29**: (sc in next 4 st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[28\]

**RND 30**: (sc in next 5 st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[30\]

**RND 31**: (sc in next 6 st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[32\]

**RND 32**: (sc in next 7 st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[34\]

**RND 33**: (sc in next 8 st, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st) \* repeat 2 times \[36\] *(pic.17)*

**RND 34-39**: sc in next 36 st \[36\] - *6 rounds*

**RND 40**: sc in next 34 st *(pic.18)* *(+ - 1 st, it should be in the middle of the eyes). Do not finish this round.*  
*Don't fasten off, continue working with a pattern for the leg 1.*

## Leg 1

**RND 1**: sc in next 18 st, *skip next 18 st* \[18\]

**RND 2**: *start in the first stitch of the previous round (pic.19),* sc in next 18 st *(pic.20)* \[18\]

**RND 3**: (dec, sc in next 7 st) \* repeat 2 times \[16\] *(pic.21)*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-mouse/12.jpg)

**RND 4**: sc in next 16 st \[16\]

**RND 5**: (dec, sc in next 6 st) \* repeat 2 times \[14\]

**RND 6**: sc in next 14 st \[14\]

**RND 7**: (dec, sc in next 5 st) \* repeat 2 times \[12\] *(pic.22)*

**RND 8**: dec 6 times \[6\] *(pic.23)*  
*Change yarn to the pink.*

**RND 9-10**: sc in next 6 st \[6\] - *2 rounds (pic.24-25)*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-mouse/13.jpg)

***Work in rows.***

**ROW 11**: ch 1 *(pic.26)*, *turn the detail (pic.27)*, sc in next 3 st *(pic.28)*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-mouse/14.jpg)

**ROW 12**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 3 st *(pic.29)*  

***Work in rounds.***

**RND 13**: sc in all 6 st around the detail \[6\] *(pic.30)*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-mouse/15.jpg)

**RND 14-16**: sc in next 6 st \[6\] - *3 rounds (pic.31-32)*  
*Stuff the pink part of the leg.*  
Make 1 sc*, then fold the circle to a semicircle.* Make sc in next 2 st through both layers*. (pic.33)*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-mouse/16.jpg)

*Fasten off and weave in the yarn ends. (pic.34)*  
*Stuff the body and gray part of the leg. (pic.35)*  

## Leg 2

*Start crochet with gray yarn.*  
*Start crochet in the first skipped stitch in Rnd 1 of the Leg 1. (pic.36)*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-mouse/17.jpg)

**RND 1**: sc in next 18 st \[18\]

**RND 2**: *start in first st of the previous rnd,* sc in next 18 st \[18\]

**RND 3**: (dec, sc in next 7 st) \* repeat 2 times \[16\]

**RND 4**: sc in next 16 st \[16\]

**RND 5**: (dec, sc in next 6 st) \* repeat 2 times \[14\]

**RND 6**: sc in next 14 st \[14\]

**RND 7**: (dec, sc in next 5 st) \* repeat 2 times \[12\]  
*Stuff the leg.*

**RND 8**: dec 6 times \[6\]  
*Change yarn to the pink.*

**RND 9-10**: sc in next 6 st \[6\] - *2 rounds*  
***Work in rows.***

**ROW 11**: sc in next 4 st, ch 1, *turn the detail*

**ROW 12**: sc in next 3 st, ch 1, *turn the detail*  

***Work in rounds.***

**RND 13**: sc in all 6 st around the detail \[6\]

**RND 14-16**: sc in next 6 st \[6\] - *3 rounds*  
*Stuff the pink part of the leg.*  
Make 1 sc*, then f**old the circle to a semicircle.* Make sc in next 2 st through both layers.  
*Fasten off and weave in the yarn ends.*  

## Nose

*Crochet in gray yarn.*

**RND 1**: 2 ch, 6 sc in second ch from a hook \[6\]

**RND 2**: (sc in next st, inc in next st) \* repeat 3 times \[9\]

**RND 3**: (sc in next 2 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 3 times \[12\]

**RND 4**: (sc in next 3 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 3 times \[15\]

**RND 5**: (sc in next 4 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 3 times \[18\]

RND 6: (sc in next 5 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 3 times \[21\] *(pic.39)*  
*Fasten off, leave a long tail for sewing.*  
*Stuff the detail.*  
*Sew the detail. Embroider a little nose with pink yarn.*

## Arms

*Make 2.*  
*Start crochet with pink yarn.*

**RND 1**: 2 ch *(pic.40)*, 6 sc in second ch from a hook \[6\] *(pic.41)*

**RND 2**: (inc in next st, sc in next 2 st) \* repeat 2 times \[8\] *(pic.42)*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-mouse/19.jpg)

**RND 3**: sc in next 8 st \[8\]

**RND 4**: (dec, sc in next 2 st) \* repeat 2 times \[6\] *(pic.43)*

**RND 5-15**: sc in next 6 st \[6\] - *11 rounds*

## Ears

*Make 2.*  
*Crochet with gray yarn.*

**RND 1**: 2 ch, 6 sc in second ch from a hook \[6\]

**RND 2**: inc in all 6 st \[12\]

**RND 3**: (sc in next st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[18\] *(pic.46)*

**RND 4-7**: sc in next 18 st \[18\] - *4 rounds (pic.47)*  
*The ears do not need to be stuffed.*  
*Fasten off, leave a long tail for sewing.*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-mouse/21.jpg)

## Tail

*Crochet in pink yarn.*  
2 ch, 6 sc in second ch from a hook \[6\]  
Crochet 6 sc in a round till needed length of tail.  
*The tail does not need to be stuffed.*  
*Fasten off, leave a long tail for sewing. Sew the tail.*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-mouse/22.jpg)

## Knitted hat

*Knit with yellow yarn.*  

Knit a sample (5 x 5 cm) with your knitting needles and yarn to calculate how many stitches you will need for your circumference.  
Pattern - (knit two, purl two) \* repeat to the end of the row. Repeat this row until the end.  

19 cm head circumference  
11 stitches in 5 cm  
So for 19 cm, you will need 42 stitches  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-mouse/23.jpg)

Cast on 42 st.

ROW 1: (K2, P2) \* repeat to end

ROW 2: (K2, P2) \* repeat to end  
Repeat row 2 until work measures needed hat's height.  
Cast off, leaving a long tail.  
With yarn needle - insert a tail of yarn through all stitches on the top of the detail and pull it. *(pic.53-54)*  
Sew together two sides of the hat (with a yarn needle).  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-mouse/24.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-mouse/25.jpg)

*Put on the hat and then sew ears to the head.*  

## Christmas edition

### Gift

*Crochet with red yarn.*  
*Make 6 pieces.*

**ROW 1**: 8 ch *(pic.55)*, *start in the second ch from a hook*, sl st in next 7 ch \[7\] *(pic.56)*

**ROW 2-8**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, BLO sl st in next 7 st \[7\] - *7 rounds (pic.58)*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-mouse/26.jpg)

*Fasten off.*  
*Sew all pieces together in a gift box (with yarn needle or crochet hook and slip stitches) and stuff it. (pic.59-60)*  

![](/images/patterns/little-crochet-mouse/27.jpg)

*With white yarn* make 100 ch, then make sl st in each stitch *starting from the second ch from a hook*. *(pic.61)*  

*Decorate the gift box with white detail.*  
*Decorate the tail with a red ribbon.*  
*Put on the hat and then sew ears to the head.*
