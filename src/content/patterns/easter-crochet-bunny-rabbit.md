Hello!  
Here you can find a quick and easy free crochet pattern of the Easter rabbit.  
I was so inspired by the Lindt chocolate easter bunny that I decided to repeat it as a crochet toy!  
I hope you will like it too!  

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/03.jpg)

**ABBREVIATION**  
*The pattern is written in English, using US crochet terminology.*  

ch - chain  
sc - single crochet  
inc - increase  
dec - decrease  
sc3tog - single crochet three together decrease  
rsc - reverse single crochet  

Happy crocheting!  

## Main detail

Ch 20 *(pic.1)*.

**ROW 1**: *start in the second ch from a hook (pic.2)*, sc in next 19 ch \[19\] *(pic.3)*  

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/04.jpg)

**ROW 2**: ch 1*(pic.7)*, *turn the detail (pic.8)*, inc in next st *(pic.4-6)*, sc in next 17 st, inc in next st \[21\] *(pic.9)*  

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/05.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/06.jpg)

**ROW 3**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec *(pic.10-12)*, sc in next 18 st, inc in next st \[21\] *(pic.13)*  

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/07.jpg)

**ROW 4**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, inc in next st, sc in next 18 st, dec \[21\]

**ROW 5**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, inc in next st, sc in next 18 st, dec \[21\]

**ROW 6**: ch 1, *turn the detail,* dec, sc in next 18 st, inc in next st \[21\]

**ROW 7**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, inc in next st, sc in next 18 st, dec \[21\]  

**ROW 8**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 20 st, inc in next st \[22\] *(pic.14)*

**ROW 9**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 22 st \[22\]

**ROW 10**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 18 st, dec \[20\]

**ROW 11**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 16 st, dec \[18\]

**ROW 12**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 14 st, dec \[16\] *(pic.15)*  

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/08.jpg)

**ROW 13**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, inc in next st, sc in next 13 st, dec \[16\]

**ROW 14**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 13 st, inc in next st \[16\]

**ROW 15**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, inc in next st, sc in next 13 st, dec \[16\]

**ROW 16**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 13 st, inc in next st \[16\]

**ROW 17**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, inc in next st, sc in next 13 st, dec \[16\]

**ROW 18**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 14 st \[15\]  

**ROW 19-20**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 15 st \[15\] - *2 rows*

**ROW 21**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 13 st \[14\]

**ROW 22**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, inc in next st, sc in next 10 st, sc3tog *(pic.16-18)* \[13\]  

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/09.jpg)

**ROW 23**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc3tog, sc in next 9 st, inc in next st \[12\]

**ROW 24**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 10 st, dec \[11\] *(pic.19)*

**ROW 25**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 9 st \[10\]

**ROW 26-27**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 10 st \[10\] - *2 rows*

**ROW 28**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 8 st, dec \[9\]

**ROW 29**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 7 st \[8\] *(pic.20)*  

**ROW 30a**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, inc in next st, sc in next 3 st, *don't finish this row - skip next 4 st* \[5\] *(pic.21)*  

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/10.jpg)

**ROW 31a**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 3 st \[4\] *(pic.22)*

**ROW 32a**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec 2 times \[2\] *(pic.23)*  
Fasten off.  

**ROW 30b**: *start crochet in the first stitch in row 30a (pic.24)*, sc in next 4 st \[4\] *(pic.25)*  

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/11.jpg)

**ROW 31b**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 2 st \[3\]

**ROW 32b**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next st \[2\]

**ROW 33b**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 2 st \[2\]  
Fasten off. *(pic.26)*  

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/12.jpg)

## Joining the pieces

Make 2 main details. *(pic.27)*  

Place both details together, matching up all sides. *(pic.28)*  
Begin attaching details together with reverse sc around. *(pic.29-36)*  

How to crochet reverse single crochet stitch [https://youtu.be/RLr4eIkrJzg](https://youtu.be/RLr4eIkrJzg)  

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/13.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/14.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/15.jpg)

Stop in the middle of the last side, stuff the detail *(pic.37-38)*. Stuff slightly.  

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/16.jpg)

Continue crochet rsc and close with rsc in first st and fasten off, weave in yarn ends. *(pic.39)*  

## Shaping the paws, tail and ears

Now the most important part!  

Highlight the legs, tail, ears, and head.  

[https://youtu.be/yTjWR0FuT\_I](https://youtu.be/yTjWR0FuT_I)  

We need a needle and a piece of additional thread (see in "you will need").  

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/17.jpg)

Make a knot at the end of the thread. Insert the needle from the back and draw it up in point 1 *(pic.40,41)*.  

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/18.jpg)

Insert needle near this point and output from the other side of the toy, pull tightly. *(pic.42-44)*  

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/19.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/20.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/21.jpg)

Then insert the needle again, and output in point 2, pull tightly. *(pic.45)*  

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/22.jpg)

Repeat this movement along the line *(pic.40)* until the paws are pronounced. *(pic.46-48)*  

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/23.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/24.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/25.jpg)

Do the same for the tail, head, and ears, follow the lines as shown on *pic.40*.  

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/26.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/27.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/28.jpg)

The bunny is almost ready! Decorate him with accessories. Also, you can embroider the eyes and nose.  

![](/images/patterns/easter-crochet-bunny-rabbit/29.jpg)
