Hello ❤︎

In this article you will find an easy amigurumi crochet pumpkin pattern, which is the best choice for beginners! 🎃

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-pumpkin/02.jpg)

## Abbreviations and crochet terminology.

*The pattern is written in English, using US terminology.*

**Skill level:** ★

*You should know how to crochet:*

**ch** - chain  
**sc** - single crochet  
**inc** - increase  
**dec** - decrease

*You also need to know how to crochet in rounds.*

**RND** - round

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-pumpkin/04.jpg)

## <a id="MainDetail"></a>Amigurumi pattern.

*We start with the main detail.*

**RND 1**: Make 2 chains. In the second chain from the hook make 6 sc \[6\]

**RND 2**: 6 inc \[12\]

**RND 3**: (1 sc, 1 inc) \* repeat 6 times \[18\]

**RND 4**: (2 sc, 1 inc) \* 6 times \[24\]

**RND 5**: (3 sc, 1 inc) \* 6 times \[30\]

**RND 6**: (4 sc, 1 inc) \* 6 times \[36\]

**RND 7**: (5 sc, 1 inc) \* 6 times \[42\]

**RND 8**: (6 sc, 1 inc) \* 6 times \[48\]

**RND 9**: (7 sc, 1 inc) \* 6 times \[54\]

**RND 10-21**: 54 sc \[54\] - *12 rounds*

**RND 22**: (7 sc, 1 dec) \* 6 times \[48\]

**RND 23**: (6 sc, 1 dec) \* 6 times \[42\]

**RND 24**: (5 sc, 1 dec) \* 6 times \[36\]

**RND 25**: (4 sc, 1 dec) \* 6 times \[30\]

**RND 26**: (3 sc, 1 dec) \* 6 times \[24\]

**RND 27**: (2 sc, 1 dec) \* 6 times \[18\]

**RND 28**: (1 sc, 1 dec) \* 6 times \[12\]

**RND 29**: 6 dec \[6\]

*Fasten off. If you are crocheting with cotton, acrylic, or wool blend yarn, leave a very long tail. You need the length to wrap around the pumpkin 4-5 times.*

*If you are crocheting with plush yarn, fasten off and weave in the yarn end. Now you need an additional cut of the cotton or acrylic yarn in the same color as plush and the length to wrap around the pumpkin 4-5 times.*

## <a id="Slices"></a>Let's make "slices"

Insert the needle into the middle of the pumpkin (where we've just finished to crochet), and pull it out from the other side (where we've started to crochet).

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-pumpkin/05.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-pumpkin/06.jpg)

Wrap the pumpkin, and again insert the needle in the same place.

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-pumpkin/07.jpg)

Pull it out on the other side and pull it tight.

In total, repeat this action 6 times, so we get 6 "slices".

*Fasten off, weave in the yarn ends.*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-pumpkin/08.jpg)

## <a id="Stalk"></a>Make a stalk

*With an additional color of yarn.*

**RND 1**: 2 ch, 6 sc in second ch from a hook \[6\]

**RND 2-4**: 6 sc \[6\] - 3 *rounds*

*Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.*

Sew the stalk to the pumpkin.

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-pumpkin/09.jpg)

## <a id="Face"></a>Face

Cut the eyes, nose, and mouth from black felt and glue them to the pumpkin.

*You also can use black half-beads as the eyes.*
