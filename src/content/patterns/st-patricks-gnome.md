**ABBREVIATIONS**

*The pattern is written in English, using US terminology.*  

ch - chain  
sc - single crochet  
inc - increase  
dec - decrease  
sl st - slip stitch  
rsc - reverse single crochet  

*You should know how to crochet: chain, slip stitch, single crochet, increase, decrease, reverse single crochet, crochet in rows and rounds.*  
Happy crocheting!  

## Body

*Crochet in dark green yarn.*  
Ch 9.

**ROW 1**: *start crochet in a second ch from a hook (pic.1)*, sc in next 8 ch \[8\] *(pic.2)*

**ROW 2**: ch 1 *(pic.3)*, *turn the detail*, inc in next st *(pic.4-6)*, sc in next 6 st, inc in next st \[10\]  

![](/images/patterns/st-patricks-gnome/04.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/st-patricks-gnome/05.jpg)

**ROW 3**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 10 st \[10\] *(pic.7)*  

**ROW 4**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, inc in next st, sc in next 8 st, inc in next st \[12\] *(pic.8)*

**ROW 5**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 12 st \[12\]

**ROW 6**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, inc in next st, sc in next 10 st, inc in next st \[14\]

**ROW 7-8**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 14 st \[14\] - *2 rows*

**ROW 9**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, inc in next st, sc in next 12 st, inc in next st \[16\]

**ROW 10-11**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 16 st \[16\] - *2 rows*

**ROW 12**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, inc in next st, sc in next 14 st, inc in next st \[18\]

**ROW 13**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 18 st \[18\]

**ROW 14**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, inc in next st, sc in next 16 st, inc in next st \[20\]

**ROW 15**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 20 st \[20\]

**ROW 16**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, inc in next st, sc in next 18 st, inc in next st \[22\]

**ROW 17-19**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 22 st \[22\] - *3 rows (pic.9)*  

![](/images/patterns/st-patricks-gnome/06.jpg)

**ROW 20**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec *(pic.10-12)*, sc in next 18 st, dec *(pic.13-14)* \[20\]  

![](/images/patterns/st-patricks-gnome/07.jpg)

**ROW 21**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 20 st \[20\] *(pic.15)*  

![](/images/patterns/st-patricks-gnome/08.jpg)

**ROW 22a**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 5 st, *do not finish this row* \[6\] *(pic.16)*

**ROW 23a**: ch 1, *turn the detail (pic.17)*, dec, sc in next 4 st \[5\] *(pic.18)*  

![](/images/patterns/st-patricks-gnome/09.jpg)

**ROW 24a**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 3 st \[4\]

**ROW 25a**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 2 st \[3\]  
*Fasten off. (pic.19)*  
***Start crochet in the last stitch of row 21, it will be the first stitch now.** (pic.20-21)*  

![](/images/patterns/st-patricks-gnome/10.jpg)

**ROW 22b**: dec *(pic.21-23)*, sc in next 5 st, *do not finish this row* \[6\] *(pic.24)*  

![](/images/patterns/st-patricks-gnome/11.jpg)

**ROW 23b**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 4 st \[5\]

**ROW 24b**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 3 st \[4\]

**ROW 25b**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, dec, sc in next 2 st \[3\]  
*Fasten off. (pic.25)*  
Make 2 pc. *(pic.26)*

Stop in the middle of the last side, stuff the detail. Continue crochet rsc and close with rsc in first st and fasten off, weave in yarn ends. *(pic. 28-41)*

## Nose

*Crochet with cream yarn.*  
Ch 4.

**RND 1**: *start crochet in a second ch from a hook (pic.42)*, sc in next 2 ch, 3 sc in next ch, *continue crochet on the other side of foundation chains (pic.43)*, sc in next st, 2 sc in next st \[8\] *(pic.44)*  

![](/images/patterns/st-patricks-gnome/18.jpg)

**RND 2**: inc in next st, sc in next 2 st, inc in next 2 st, sc in next 2 st, inc in next st \[12\] *(pic.45)*

**RND 3**: sc in next 12 st \[12\]

**RND 4**: dec, sc in next 2 st, dec 2 times, sc in next 2 st, dec \[8\]  
*Stuff the detail*. *(pic.46)*  

**RND 5**: dec 4 times \[4\]  
*Fasten off, leave a long tail for sewing. (pic.47)*  

![](/images/patterns/st-patricks-gnome/19.jpg)

Then sew the nose between rounds 12-15 of the body.  

## Beard

*Crochet with orange yarn.*  
For the beard, we will be branching off in chains to create beard strands.  
**STRAND 1-36**: ch 11, *start crochet in the second ch from a hook (pic.48)*, sl st in next 10 ch *(pic.49-53)*  

![](/images/patterns/st-patricks-gnome/20.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/st-patricks-gnome/21.jpg)

Then sew the beard to the body.*(pic.54-59)*  

![](/images/patterns/st-patricks-gnome/22.jpg)

## Hat

*Start crochet with dark green yarn.*  
Ch 19. *(pic.60)*

**ROW 1**: *start crochet in the second ch from a hook (pic.61)*, sc in next 18 ch \[18\] *(pic.62)*  

![](/images/patterns/st-patricks-gnome/23.jpg)

**ROW 2-11**: ch 1 *(pic.63)*, *turn the detail (pic.64)*, sc in next 18 st \[18\] - *10 rows (pic.65-66)*  

![](/images/patterns/st-patricks-gnome/24.jpg)

**ROW 12**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, 4 sc in next st *(pic.67)*, sc in next 16 st, 4 sc in next st \[24\] *(pic.68)*  

![](/images/patterns/st-patricks-gnome/25.jpg)

**ROW 13**: ch 1, *turn the detail (pic.69)*, 4 sc in next st *(pic.70)*, sc in next 22 st, 4 sc in next st \[30\] *(pic.71)*  

![](/images/patterns/st-patricks-gnome/26.jpg)

**ROW 14**: ch 1, *turn the detail*, sc in next 30 st \[30\]  
*Fasten off, weave in yarn ends. (pic.72)*  

Make 2 pc.  

Place both details together, matching up all sides. *(pic.73)*  
Begin attaching details together with reverse sc around, using light green color yarn. (pic.74-82)  

![](/images/patterns/st-patricks-gnome/27.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/st-patricks-gnome/28.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/st-patricks-gnome/29.jpg)

*With black yarn* crochet 46 ch *(pic.83)*, *starting in a second ch from a hook*, crochet sl st in next 45 ch. Close with sl st in the first stitch *(pic.84)*. Fasten off, weave in yarn ends. Sew the detail to the hat. *(pic.85)*  

![](/images/patterns/st-patricks-gnome/30.jpg)

*With yellow yarn* crochet 11 ch, then make sl st in the first ch. Fasten off, weave in yarn ends. *(pic.86)* Sew to the hat.
