Hello! Welcome to my blog full of the free crochet and amigurumi patterns!

On this page, you find a very detailed and easy to follow tutorial for the crochet radish.

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/02.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/03.jpg)

**Abbreviations**

*The pattern is written in English, using US terminology.*

**ch** - chain

**sc** - single crochet

**sl st** - slip stitch

**inc** - increase

**dec** - (invisible) decrease

**hdc** - half double crochet

**dc** - double crochet

*You should know how to crochet: chains, slip stitches, single crochet stitches, increases and decreases, half double crochet and double crochet stitches, how to crochet in rounds*.

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/04.jpg)

## Main detail

*Start crochet with red yarn.*

**RND 1**: Ch 2, 6 sc in second ch from a hook \[6\] */pic.1-2/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/07.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/08.jpg)

**RND 2**: inc in next 6 st \[12\] */pic.3/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/09.jpg)

**RND 3**: (sc in next st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[18\] */pic.4/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/10.jpg)

**RND 4**: (sc in next 2 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[24\] */pic.5/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/11.jpg)

**RND 5**: (sc in next 3 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[30\] */pic.6/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/12.jpg)

**RND 6-9**: sc in next 30 st \[30\] - *4 rounds /pic.7/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/13.jpg)

**RND 10**: (sc in next 3 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[24\] */pic.8/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/14.jpg)

**RND 11**: sc in next 24 st \[24\] */pic.9/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/15.jpg)

*Change yarn to the white.*

Starting this round don't count the stitches in the rounds, just follow the pattern!

**RND 12**: (sc in next 3 st, dec) \* repeat 5 times */pic.10/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/16.jpg)

**RND 13**: (sc in next 2 st, dec) \* repeat 5 times */pic.11/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/17.jpg)

*Stuff the detail. /pic.12/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/18.jpg)

**RND 14**: (sc in next 2 st, dec) \* repeat 4 times */pic.13/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/19.jpg)

**RND 15**: dec 6 times */pic.14-15/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/20.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/21.jpg)

Make 12 chains and then fasten off, leaving a tail of yarn approx. 2" (5 cm) */pic.16-17/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/22.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/23.jpg)

*Crochet with green yarn.*

1) Make 20 chains */pic.18/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/24.jpg)

2) sc in the second ch from a hook */pic.19-21/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/25.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/26.jpg)

3) hdc in next ch */pic.21-22/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/27.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/28.jpg)

4) dc in next 6 ch */pic.23/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/29.jpg)

5) hdc in next ch */pic.24/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/30.jpg)

6) 3 sc in next ch */pic.25/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/31.jpg)

7) *turn the detail and continue crochet on the other side of foundation chains (skip the next 10 chains) /pic.26/*

8) hdc in next ch */pic.26-27/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/32.jpg)

9) dc in next 6 ch */pic.27-28/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/33.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/34.jpg)

10) hdc in next ch

11) sc in next ch */pic.29/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/35.jpg)

Turn the detail and continue crochet in the middle of the leaf, where the foundation chains. */pic.30/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/36.jpg)

Crochet the slip stitches to the end of the leaf */pic.31-39/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/37.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/38.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/39.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/40.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/41.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/42.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/43.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/44.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/45.jpg)

Continue crochet the slip stitches follow the skipped chains */pic.40-43/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/46.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/47.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/48.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/49.jpg)

Don't fasten off and continue crochet 3 more leaves */pic.44/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/50.jpg)

For the second leaf make 25 chains */pic.45-46/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/51.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/52.jpg)

for the third leaf make 30 chains */pic.47-48/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/53.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/54.jpg)

for the fourth leaf make 15 chains */pic.49-50/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/55.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-radish/56.jpg)

When all leaves are done, fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing. And then sew the leaves to the main detail */pic.51-53/*
