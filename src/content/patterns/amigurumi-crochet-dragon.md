Hello ❤︎

On this page, you will find a free crochet dragon pattern.

This pattern is one of the amigurumi patterns inspired by the Chinese horoscope. Patterns from this series that have already been published can be found by this link ↠ [Chinese horoscope patterns](/collections/chinese-horoscope/)

In 2020 I first came up with the dragon idea. Look at the sketches! I decided not to wait for the year of the dragon, according to the Chinese horoscope, and made a pattern immediately!

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-crochet-dragon/02.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-crochet-dragon/03.jpg)

I look forward to seeing your photos of the amigurumi dragon on Instagram with the hashtag [#knittedstorybears](https://www.instagram.com/explore/tags/knittedstorybears/) !

## Terms and stitches.

*The pattern is written in English, using US terminology.*

**ch** - chain  
**sc** - single crochet  
**inc** - increase  
**dec** - decrease

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-crochet-dragon/21.jpg)

**RND 21**: (4 sc, 1 dec) \* 6 times \[30\]

**RND 22**: (3 sc, 1 dec) \* 6 times \[24\]

Start to stuff the head. */pic.20/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-crochet-dragon/24.jpg)

**RND 23**: (2 sc, 1 dec) \* 6 times \[18\]

**RND 24**: 18 sc \[18\]

**RND 25**: (1 inc, 8 sc) \* 2 times \[20\]

**RND 26**: (1 sc, 1 inc, 8 sc) \* 2 times \[22\]

Stuff the head to the end. */pic.21-22/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-crochet-dragon/25.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-crochet-dragon/26.jpg)

**RND 27**: (2 sc, 1 inc, 8 sc) \* 2 times \[24\]

**RND 28**: (3 sc, 1 inc, 8 sc) \* 2 times \[26\]

**RND 29**: (4 sc, 1 inc, 8 sc) \* 2 times \[28\]

**RND 30**: (5 sc, 1 inc, 8 sc) \* 2 times \[30\]

**RND 31**: (6 sc, 1 inc, 8 sc) \* 2 times \[32\]

**RND 32**: (7 sc, 1 inc, 8 sc) \* 2 times \[34\]

**RND 33**: (8 sc, 1 inc, 8 sc) \* 2 times \[36\] */pic.23/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-crochet-dragon/27.jpg)

**RND 34-38**: 36 sc \[36\] -- 5 rounds

**RND 39**: 34 sc (+ - 1 st, the last stitch should be in the middle of the head), don't finish this round */pic.24-25/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-crochet-dragon/28.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-crochet-dragon/29.jpg)

Don't fasten off, continue working on a first leg.

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-crochet-dragon/33.jpg)

**RND 4**: 16 sc \[16\]

**RND 5**: (1 dec, 6 sc) \* 2 times \[14\]

**RND 9**: (1 dec, 4 sc) \* 2 times \[10\]

**RND 10**: 10 sc \[10\]

**RND 11**: (1 dec, 3 sc) \* 2 times \[8\]

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-crochet-dragon/34.jpg)

**RND 12**: 4 inc, 4 sc \[12\]

**RND 13**: 8 sc, 2 dec \[10\]

Stuff the leg to the end. */pic.31/*

Fasten off, leaving a long tail approx. 6 inches (15 cm). */pic.32/*

## <a id="SecondLeg"></a>Second leg.

Start crochet in the first skipped stitch in RND 1 of the first leg. */pic.40-41/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-crochet-dragon/47.jpg)

**RND 2**: start in the first stitch of the previous round */pic.43/*, 18 sc \[18\]

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-crochet-dragon/48.jpg)

**RND 3**: (1 dec,7 sc) \* 2 times \[16\]

**RND 4**: 16 sc \[16\]

**RND 5**: (1 dec, 6 sc) \* 2 times \[14\]

**RND 6**: 14 sc \[14\]

**RND 7**: (1 dec, 5 sc) \* 2 times \[12\]

**RND 8**: 12 sc \[12\]

**RND 9**: (1 dec, 4 sc) \* 2 times \[10\]

**RND 10**: 10 sc \[10\]

Stuff the leg. */pic.44/*

![](/images/patterns/amigurumi-crochet-dragon/49.jpg)

**RND 11**: (1 dec, 3 sc) \* 2 times \[8\]

**RND 12**: 4 sc, 4 inc \[12\]

**RND 13**: 2 dec, 8 sc \[10\]

**RND 14**: 2 sc, 1 dec, 4 sc, 1 dec \[8\]

Stuff the leg to the end.

**RND 15**: 4 dec \[4\]

Fasten off, leaving a long tail approx. 6 inches (15 cm).

With a yarn needle, insert the tail of the yarn through the front loops of all stitches of the last round. Pull all together at once.
