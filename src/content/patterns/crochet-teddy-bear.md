**Abbreviation:**  
ch - chain  
sc - single crochet  
inc - increase  
dec - decrease  
FLO - front loops only  
BLO - back loops only  
sl st - slip stitch  
\[number\] - a number of stitches in the round  

Happy crocheting!  

## Head

Crochet with beige yarn.

**Rnd 1**: Ch 2, 6 sc in second ch from hook \[6\]  

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/04.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/05.jpg)

**Rnd 2**: inc in all 6 st \[12\]  

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/06.jpg)

**Rnd 3**: (sc in next st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[18\]

**Rnd 4**: (sc in next 2 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[24\]

**Rnd 5**: (sc in next 3 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[30\]

**Rnd 6**: (sc in next 4 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[36\]

**Rnd 7**: (sc in next 5 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[42\]

**Rnd 8-13**: sc in all 42 st \[42\] - 6 rounds  

**Rnd 14**: (sc in next 5 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[36\]

**Rnd 15**: (sc in next 4 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[30\]

**Rnd 16**: (sc in next 3 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[24\]  
*Stuff the head with fiberfill.*  

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/07.jpg)

**Rnd 17**: (sc in next 2 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[18\]

**Rnd 18**: (sc in next st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[12\]

**Rnd 19**: dec 6 times \[6\]  
Fasten off and weave in the yarn end.  

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/08.jpg)

## Body

Start in beige yarn.

**Rnd 1**: Ch 2, 6 sc in second ch from hook \[6\]

**Rnd 2**: inc in all 6 st \[12\]  

**Rnd 3**: (sc in next st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[18\]

**Rnd 4**: (sc in next 2 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[24\]

**Rnd 5**: (sc in next 3 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[30\]

**Rnd 6**: (sc in next 4 st, inc in next st) \* repeat 6 times \[36\]

**Rnd 7-9**: sc in all 36 st \[36\] - 3 rounds  
Change to bright yarn.  

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/09.jpg)

**Rnd 10**: FLO sc in all 36 st \[36\]

**Rnd 11**: BLO (of rnd 9) sc in all 36 st \[36\]

**Rnd 12**: (into both loops of rnd 11) sc in all 36 st \[36\]  

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/10.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/11.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/12.jpg)

**Rnd 13-15**: sc in all 36 st \[36\] - 3 rounds  

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/13.jpg)

**Rnd 16**: (sc in next 4 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[30\]

**Rnd 17**: sc in all 30 st \[30\]

**Rnd 18**: (sc in next 3 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[24\]

**Rnd 19**: sc in all 24 st \[24\]  
*Stuff the body with fiberfill.*  

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/14.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/15.jpg)

**Rnd 20**: (sc in next 2 st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[18\]

**Rnd 21**: (sc in next st, dec) \* repeat 6 times \[12\]

**Rnd 22**: dec 6 times \[6\]  
Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.  

## Arms (make 2)

Start in beige yarn.

**Rnd 1**: Ch 2, 5 sc in second ch from hook \[5\]

**Rnd 2**: inc in all 5 st \[10\]

**Rnd 3-4**: sc in all 10 st \[10\] - 2 rounds  
Change to bright yarn.

**Rnd 5-12**: sc in all 10 st \[10\] - 8 rounds  
*The arms do not need to be stuffed.*  
Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.  

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/16.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/17.jpg)

## Legs (make 2)

Crochet with beige yarn.

**Rnd 1**: Ch 2, 5 sc in second ch from hook \[5\]

**Rnd 2**: inc in all 5 st \[10\]

**Rnd 3-5**: sc in all 10 st \[10\] - 3 rounds  
*The legs do not need to be stuffed.*  
Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.  

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/18.jpg)

## Ears (make 2)

Crochet with beige yarn.

**Rnd 1**: Ch 2, 6 sc in second ch from hook \[6\]

**Rnd 2**: inc in all 6 st \[12\]

**Rnd 3-4**: sc in all 12 st \[12\] - 2 rounds  
*The ears do not need to be stuffed.*  
Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.  

## Assembly

\- sew the ears to the head;  
\- sew the body to the head;  
\- sew the arms to the body;  
\- sew the legs to the body;  

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/19.jpg)

\- crochet a collar with yellow yarn:  
*Row 1*: Ch 5, start in the second ch from hook, sc in next 4 ch. Ch 1, turn the detail.  
*Row 2* and all next for needed length of collar: BLO sc in all 4 st, Ch 1, turn the detail.  
Wrap the collar around the neck, insert hook through loops of the first row and last row, make sc in all 4 st, Fasten off and weave in the yarn end.  

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/20.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/21.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/22.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/23.jpg)

\- embroider the nose with brown yarn;  
\- glue the eyes to the head.  

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/24.jpg)

![](/images/patterns/crochet-teddy-bear/25.jpg)
